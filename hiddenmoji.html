About HiddenMoji

HiddenMoji transforms ordinary messages into mysterious emoji combinations using modern encryption. Encrypt a message, share the emoji code, and let someone with the password reveal what it says.

How to use: Encrypt with a password, send the emoji code, then share the password separately through a safe channel. The receiver pastes the code into the SECRET tab and decrypts it with the same password.
